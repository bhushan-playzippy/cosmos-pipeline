## 3. Content gaps for the "everyday comparison, Indian references" angle

These are opportunities I inferred, not measured.

**Indian scale references**
- Scale the Solar System to Indian distances (Mumbai to Delhi, Kanyakumari to Kashmir).
- Use local objects for size: a cricket ball, a cricket pitch (22 yards), the Wankhede stadium.
- Use local measures for volume and density: a teaspoon of neutron star, a matka, a tiffin.

**ISRO missions retold through comparisons**
- Chandrayaan-3's landing.
- Aditya-L1's distance to L1.
- Mangalyaan's cost compared with a film budget. This is a widely repeated claim; **verify it before using it.**
- Gaganyaan's orbit altitude compared with a Mumbai–Pune drive.

**Exam-friendly "kya hai" explainers in English with Indian context:** light-year, Lagrange point, escape velocity.

**Rule:** check every number against ISRO or NASA primary pages before publishing, and keep a per-video fact sheet.

## 6. Risks

- **AI-slop perception and policy**
  - A **[third-party]** Kapwing study found 21% of the first 500 videos served to a new account were AI-generated [K1].
  - YouTube clarified its policy on 2026-07-16: generic, template-based AI content can't be monetized.
  - **TTS + still images + templated scripts is the highest-risk profile.**
  - Mitigations:
    - Vary the formats.
    - Make original diagrams and animations.
    - Have a human edit every script.
    - Give each video a distinct narrative arc.
    - Add a visible "sources" line.
- **Factual errors**
  - Gemini can hallucinate numbers, and science audiences correct mistakes publicly in the comments.
  - Mitigations:
    - Keep a per-script fact table with a NASA, ISRO or ESA URL for each figure.
    - Round honestly ("about"), and avoid outdated "facts".
- **Saturation**
  - Large Hindi fact channels (FactTechz 21.6M, Antariksh TV 5.38M) and English giants already exist.
  - The "Cosmic Scale" name is crowded (section 0).
  - The differentiator has to be the **Indian-reference comparison** plus accuracy.
- **Economics**
  - Shorts-only revenue at Indian RPMs is tiny **[est.]**, and the 2027 bar is higher (20M Shorts views per 90 days for new applicants).
  - Plan for long-form compilations or a 2027+ horizon.
- **Channel history:** repurposing the former psychology channel mixes topical signals. Reviewers look at the channel's main theme and newest videos. **Consider whether old videos should be removed or kept.** I did not research the impact of either choice. **[unverified]**
